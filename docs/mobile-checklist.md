# Phone security checklist

DeviceSentinel cannot inspect Android or iOS internals. For a phone, review these settings directly in the system Settings app:

- Install operating-system and app updates.
- Use a strong device passcode and enable the platform's built-in biometric unlock if appropriate.
- Confirm Find My / Find My Device is enabled and recovery details are current.
- Review installed apps and remove ones you do not recognize or use.
- Review account sign-in activity and revoke unknown sessions.
- Avoid installing configuration profiles, certificates, or apps from links you did not expect.
- Contact the mobile carrier if you suspect SIM swapping; secure the carrier account with a PIN.

These steps reduce risk but do not prove that a phone is uncompromised. If compromise is suspected, use the vendor's official support and account recovery guidance from a separate trusted device.
